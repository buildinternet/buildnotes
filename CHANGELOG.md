# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and releases will follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

Until the first release, changes are recorded under [Unreleased].

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Brands: a per-repo `brand.json` next to `house-style.md` (colours by role in light and dark, display, body and mono fonts with a stylesheet, and a logo) that the post, the motion cut and the deck follow. `scripts/brand.mjs` checks it (unknown keys, hex colours, and a contrast warning below 4.5:1), derives the roles it implies, and writes it into a page as a style block after the template's own, so pages without a brand are unchanged. `viz.mjs build`, `motion.mjs` and `deck.mjs` apply it; `motion.json` takes `"brand"` overrides, and `--brand`/`--no-brand` pick another file or none. `workdir.mjs` prints its path. Docs in `references/visuals.md#brand`, `motion.md`, `deck.md` and `SKILL.md`; tests in `dev/test/brand.test.mjs`.
- The buildnotes skill (named build-story until 2026-10-15): `SKILL.md` and the references in `references/` (brief, evidence, story, visuals, modules, craft). It turns issues, pull requests, commits, reviews, and screenshots into a post, in three flavors (release story, build story, and technical deep dive), and it says when a change is only a note.
- `scripts/gather.mjs`, which writes `dossier.md` and `dossier.json` from `git` and `gh`: issues, pull requests, commits, review threads, nearby work, and prior work.
- Visual components in `scripts/viz.mjs` (flow, bars, delta, line, options, matrix, code, term, strip, steps, shader), the page template in `assets/template.html`, and the component gallery.
- `scripts/lint.mjs`, `scripts/render.mjs`, `scripts/export.mjs`, `scripts/inline-assets.mjs`, `scripts/module.mjs`, `scripts/workdir.mjs`, and `scripts/chrome.mjs`.
- `examples/releases-weekly-digests`, a committed run against a public repository.
- Install paths for Claude Code, OpenCode, and Codex, and the MIT license, including the notice for the capture script adapted from [anatomy](https://github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy).
- Node.js 22 as the minimum (`package.json` engines, the README, and `SKILL.md`). GitHub Actions checks script syntax, `SKILL.md` frontmatter, and the test suite on Node 22 ([#1](https://github.com/buildinternet/buildnotes/pull/1)).
- This changelog, a pull request template that asks for an Unreleased line, and fixture tests for `scripts/gather.mjs` (`dev/test/gather.test.mjs`, `dev/test/fixtures/gather/`).
- Two page styles in `assets/template.html`: Plain, the default, and field notes (`data-style="field-notes"` on the article), each in light and dark. Colour tokens now have roles, with `--landmark` added for landmarks and target lines, and `--font-label` and `--font-code` for labels and code. `viz.mjs build` reads the style and draws flows by hand in field notes, seeded so every build matches (`--style` for a single figure, `dev/gallery.mjs` and `dev/shots.mjs`). The styles and the colour roles are in `references/visuals.md#styles`.

- Template components: a `finding` (one sentence a chapter found), margin notes (`<span class="sidenote">`, in the right margin on wide screens), and keyed figures (numbered hotspots on a screenshot with a legend; the page's script links the two and shows one note at a time). Screenshots in `.shot`, `.strip` and keyed figures open full size in a shared dialog when drawn smaller than their real size. `lint.mjs` checks keyed figures (hotspots and legend match, positions set, at most about six), findings (one per chapter) and margin-note length. Docs in `references/story.md` and `references/visuals.md`.

- The `anatomy` component (`scripts/viz/anatomy.mjs`): the parts of one thing, named. One parts list, measured on a capture, draws as keys over the capture (`screenshot`, with an optional dark capture), a flat drawing with the object's own labels (`flat`), or an angled drawing of its structure that comes apart on request (`iso`). Each renders as a keyed figure; pointing at a part lifts it, and pointing at a key fades the rest. A spec can start from another with `from`. Example specs in `assets/specs/anatomy-card*.json`, measured on the shipped digest card; tests in `dev/test/anatomy.test.mjs`.
- `examples/releases-weekly-digests/post-field-notes.html`, the example in the field notes style, and `examples/releases-weekly-digests/viz/`, the specs behind its rendered figures.
- A command line in the template, `<div class="cmd"><code>…</code></div>`, for a command the reader will copy: a bar with a copy button, the command name in full ink, flags quieter and quoted strings in the accent. A long command scrolls inside the bar with a fade at the edge. `lint.mjs` points at a one-line `<pre>` that should be one. Docs in `references/visuals.md#components`.
- The motion cut, an optional step 9: the post as a reel of five to nine scenes that plays itself in under a minute, for people who would rather watch than read. The agent writes a scene script (`motion.json`); `scripts/motion.mjs` checks it, sets each scene's length from its motion and its words, builds one self-contained page from `assets/motion.html`, and exports a 1920×1080 MP4, WebM, GIF or still through `export.mjs`. Nine scene kinds: title, statement, list, shot, swap, bars, flow, morph and end. Docs in `references/motion.md`; the weekly-digests example has its cut in `motion.json` and `motion.html`; tests in `dev/test/motion.test.mjs`.
- Motion cut clips: `motion.mjs --scene <n>` exports one scene as a short clip and `--size <px>` sets the export width. The README has a Motion cut section with four clips from the weekly-digests cut (`.github/assets/motion-*.gif`) and the full video, and the example keeps its export as `examples/releases-weekly-digests/motion.mp4` (1280×720, 0.8 MB).
- `examples/sunny-walkthrough-videos`, a second committed example: a field-notes build story about walkthrough videos in Sunny (a private repo, so the post quotes no code and links nothing internal), with its plan, notes, specs and a 52-second motion cut (`motion.json`, `motion.html`, `motion.mp4`). The README's Example section is now Examples, with both runs.
- `examples/uploads-before-after`, a third committed example: a Plain-style build story about before/after comparison on uploads.sh (public repo `buildinternet/uploads`), with figures captured from the product in use (a real pull request comment and the live file page in Compare) and a 47-second motion cut whose `clip` scene is a recording of the divider sweeping across a real pair. The motion cut's `shot` scene now holds every region for 2.2 s; the first used to leave after about a second.
- A `clip` scene kind for the motion cut: the feature's own GIF or video, trimmed with `from` and `to` and sped up with `speed`, turned into one sheet of frames with `ffmpeg` at build time and stepped by the reel's clock, so it exports frame-exact. Bars take a `unit`, and a value keeps its own decimals; the label column fits the longest label. `"sayAt": "top"` moves a `clip` or `swap` label to the top corner.
- `docs/install.md`, `docs/scripts.md` and `docs/developing.md`, split out of the README, which now keeps the overview, examples and components. `SKILL.md` and `docs/install.md` give a fallback when `gh` isn't installed: a GitHub MCP server, then public GitHub pages for a public repo, then commits alone.
- `gather.mjs` without `gh`: git-only runs read the repo from the `origin` remote instead of crashing, `--issue`, `--pr` and `--search` exit 2 with one line pointing at [Without the GitHub CLI](docs/install.md#without-the-github-cli), and `--visibility public|private` records a visibility confirmed elsewhere, such as through a GitHub MCP server.
- `site/`: the project's one-page website. `node site/build.mjs` builds it into `site/dist/`, with two live figures rendered from the examples by `viz.mjs`, pictures drawn in the anatomy style by `site/iso.mjs`, a copy button on every command, and link-preview tags (description, Open Graph, Twitter) with a 1200 × 630 card made by `site/card.mjs`. See [Developing](docs/developing.md#website).
- A 40-second promo video for social posts, `site/assets/promo.mp4`, in the skill's own output styles (the motion cut's title card and the field-notes page). `site/promo/build.mjs` builds it from the site's drawings and the walkthrough-videos example, and exports it with the skill's own `export.mjs`.
- A deck, the last output on the site's list: `scripts/deck.mjs` builds the motion cut's scene script as slides you click through (`deck.html`, one scene to a slide, each playing once and then holding; arrow keys, a counter, `#3` links, full screen) and, with `--pdf`, a 16:9 PDF with one page per slide. `deck` works as the request's first word. Deck mode lives in `assets/motion.html`, so the reel and the deck share every scene. Docs in `references/deck.md`; every example has `deck.html` and `deck.pdf`; tests in `dev/test/deck.test.mjs`.

### Changed

- `anatomy`: the flat and screenshot views put each key's text in the margin, joined to its part by a hairline (a bracket for a key with `"span": true`, a dot otherwise), instead of numbered discs and a legend. Below about 560px they fall back to small numbers and the legend; `"notes": "keys"` keeps the old hotspots, and iso always uses them. The flat view takes the iso view's tones with an offset shadow, and its list rows are drawn as raised slabs. The weekly-digests card on the site is drawn flat instead of iso. `lint.mjs` counts margin notes as hotspots, and `inline-assets.mjs` and the gallery handle an SVG `<image href>`.

- Length guidance sizes a post by the scope of the change, with bands measured against published Cloudflare and Astro posts (`references/story.md`). The deep dive gets a soft ceiling of about 3,000 words: `lint.mjs` warns past it and the author can let it stand.
- Lessons from comparing two outside deep dives with the projects' own announcements: stage a cause a reader would hit in a demo run and show the fix (`references/evidence.md`), read a source's claim in its context before calling it wrong (`references/evidence.md`), walk a report from the top down to a cause even in a deep dive, and let the try-it prove the feature's promise (`references/story.md`), and read the release a feature shipped in for where it fits (`references/brief.md`).
- The skill's argument hint and description name the deck (`[post|video|deck] …`, with a release story and a deep dive as examples), and the site's Deck card shows a `/buildnotes deck …` request like the other outputs. The home page's hero shows the request's shape under the install command: `/buildnotes [post | video | deck] what to write about`.
- Exports are named after the story: `export/<story>-fig-<n>.<ext>`, `<story>-motion.mp4`, `<story>-motion-scene-<n>.<ext>`, `<story>-deck.pdf`, where `<story>` is the work dir's name without its date (or the title). `--name <slug>` on `export.mjs`, `motion.mjs` and `deck.mjs` sets it, and so does `"output": { "name" }` in `motion.json`, which also sets the export's `theme`, `size` and `fps` once so a rebuild needs no flags. The helper is `scripts/names.mjs`.
- The website's example videos can be scrubbed: `site/worker.mjs`, the site's only Worker script, runs first for `examples/*/motion.mp4` and answers HTTP Range requests with 206 and Content-Range, which Cloudflare's static asset layer does not. The example pages end in the same column footer as the home page.
- The example folders are named after their products: `examples/sunny-walkthrough-videos`, `examples/uploads-before-after` and `examples/releases-weekly-digests`. On the website each example is a folder, `examples/<slug>/`, with its video beside it, and `examples/` lists all three with the plan behind each; the top nav links there. The weekly-digests example on the site is the field-notes page, whose first figure is the card drawn at an angle and taken apart. The home page's example block is labelled as one run, start to finish, and the footer is columns: examples, docs, project, and who maintains it.
- The website wraps each example post in a document and a site shell: a bar naming the example with a way back, and a footer with the post's video, its plan and the other examples. Until now the posts were served as committed, with no doctype or charset, so en dashes and middle dots came out as mojibake. Every example's video is served and linked from the "More examples" cards. The site is light by default, and its footer links Release Notes Index and uploads.sh.
- The README is shorter: the visual component and motion scene tables moved to [docs/components.md](docs/components.md), the examples section shows one picture per example, and the prototype note is gone from the README and the website footer. The masthead links to [buildnotes.releases.sh](https://buildnotes.releases.sh).
- The README's top image is now Fig. 1 of the weekly-digests field-notes post, the "week at a glance" card drawn at an angle with its five keys, in place of the post's masthead, which was almost all text.
- The skill moved into `skills/buildnotes/` (`SKILL.md`, `references/`, `assets/`, `scripts/`, `agents/` and a copy of the license). `npx skills add buildinternet/buildnotes` now copies that folder alone, about 1 MB, instead of the whole repo (about 16 MB, most of it `examples/` and README images). The examples, docs, tests and `dev/` stay at the root. A git checkout now links `skills/buildnotes`, not the checkout: see [Install](docs/install.md#with-git).
- The skill is renamed from `build-story` to `buildnotes`, so it runs as `/buildnotes` (`$buildnotes` in Codex). Reinstall it, or rename the folder under your skills directory. Build story is still the name of the default flavor.
- Work dirs live under `$BUILDNOTES_DIR`, default `~/.buildnotes/<repo>/`, after the rename to buildnotes. `scripts/workdir.mjs` still reads `BUILD_STORY_DIR` when the new variable is unset, and keeps using an existing `~/.build-stories` (printing the `mv` command to move it) until `~/.buildnotes` exists, so each repo's `house-style.md` is still found.
- A request can open with the output it wants: `post` (the default) or `video`, which writes the post and then its motion cut, as in `/buildnotes video tech explainer of our latest PR`. `SKILL.md` has an `argument-hint` and an Output section for this. The rest of the request is read as plain words, as before.
- Each beat now passes a reader test: what would a reader see differently if this had gone the other way? In a release or build story, a beat that only shows in the service's logs, costs or dashboards is cut. The build flavor keeps internals only where they explain something the reader sees.
- `SKILL.md` adds *understood beats exact*, and `references/evidence.md` a section on how far to check a claim: check what would mislead, and treat a claim only production data can confirm as a sign the beat is about running the service.
- Field notes sets margin notes in Source Serif 4 italic, in the note colour, instead of a handwritten face. The handwritten face (`--font-hand`, Shantell Sans) is kept for keyed-figure numbers and labels of a few words: no editorial site we checked sets sidenotes in one, and past a few words it reads slowly and re-wraps when the font loads. Margin notes have their own `--font-note` and `--note-style` tokens.
- The weekly-digests example uses the current template: the card is a keyed figure over the shipped capture, the patch-pool bars and the replay frames use the `bars` and `strip` components, chapter 2 closes with a finding, and the sentence about the card's colors now says columns, as the card has. The README shows the keyed card and the field notes version.
- `viz.mjs build` passes the page's folder to the renderers, so an image a spec names resolves relative to the page.
- `export.mjs` hides the expand icon and hotspot notes in exports, and the README's images are re-exported with it from the current gallery and example.
- Figures get more room above and below than paragraphs. A caption stays in the reading column under a figure that breaks out wider, set in the heading face a step below the text, with its figure number ("Fig. 2.") as a label; a keyed figure's legend uses the heading face too. The examples and the gallery pick this up, and the README's two keyed images are re-exported.
- `lint.mjs` counts a sentence that names a pull request as process talk only when it's about the record (a number, "the second pull request", or what was done to one), so a post about a product built around pull requests isn't flagged for describing it. The colon-reveal check flags only a short payoff after the colon.
- `gather.mjs` names a repo's changelog folder (one file per entry, such as `apps/web/src/content/changelog/`) under Prior work, and the entries the selected work added there.
- `gather.mjs` lists **Since then**: PRs merged after the selected work that touch two or more of its files, since they can change what ships today. The digest cuts a long body at a section break and prints the command to read it whole, `gather.mjs --out <dir> --show <number>`.
- Several images in one figure (a strip's frames) page with ← and → in the expanded view, with arrow buttons, a count and each frame's label.
- `render.mjs` fails an SVG label cut off at the drawing's edge, and reminds you to look at the screenshots even when every check passes.
- `references/visuals.md`: a row and a recipe for a path over a plan (computed from the product's own code), hotspot placement in a crowded corner, a screenshot key that needs no part, and how wide a hand drawing should be. `workdir.mjs` says where to start `house-style.md` when there isn't one.
- Video exports open on the poster frame: GitHub's player shows the first frame before play and ignores a poster, so the README's cuts showed a blank title card. `export.mjs --cover <s>` swaps frame 0 for that moment's frame and adds it to an MP4 as cover art; `motion.mjs` passes the poster moment for MP4 and WebM. The three example cuts are re-exported with it.

### Fixed

- The motion page declares its charset, so arrows and dots no longer come out garbled when `motion.html` is opened from disk.
- Margin notes: on a narrow screen a note is set off from the text by a rule, so it doesn't read as body text; on a wide one it sits level with its line without making the paragraph taller (it was floated, and a paragraph in the chapter grid grew to hold it, leaving a gap). `lint.mjs` flags a note placed mid-paragraph or mid-item, which splits the text on a phone; the example's one note moves to the end of its item.
- The `delta` component's before and after values lost their layout when the margin note class was renamed; the template styles `.delta .side` again.
- A keyed figure's legend lost its own spacing to the chapter's list rule (no gap above it, an indent, rows packed tight); the list rule now leaves the legend alone.
- Image paths in `strip` specs are relative to the spec, as they already were in `anatomy` specs; both now share one rule, and a path that only resolves from the page still renders, with a warning.
- `gather.mjs` no longer looks up placeholder references such as `owner/repo#123` in PR bodies.
- `workdir.mjs --help` prints its usage instead of making a folder called `--help`.
- `gather.mjs` no longer pulls a pull request in a second time as an issue when another PR says it is "part of" it (its body printed twice, and `--download` saved its images twice).
- A strip label that wraps to two lines no longer pushes its picture below the others: labels share one row.
- A hand-drawn SVG in `figure.diagram` fits the column instead of being held at 30rem and scrolling on a phone; `figure.diagram.wide` keeps the 30rem minimum.
- `lint.mjs`'s moving-dot warning says why the dot goes under the boxes in a flow, and that a route over a plan can ignore it.
- Headless Chrome gets 45 s to start instead of 20, since a busy CI runner starting several at once can take longer.
- `references/visuals.md`: how to hide sticky headers before a capture, and a crop command that honours its offset (`sips -c` crops around the centre).
- Plain replaces the template's previous look: Archivo, Newsreader and IBM Plex Mono on a white page, and figures (flows, steps, diagrams) on plain ground between two rules instead of a bordered dot grid.
