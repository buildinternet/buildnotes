Components

Every visual the skill can render from a spec, drawn by the same CSS as the page template.

Write a spec in the post's viz/ folder, put a marker where the figure goes, and run node scripts/viz.mjs build post.html. Regenerate this page with node dev/gallery.mjs --publish.

11anatomy

The parts of one thing, named: a keyed figure drawn from a parts list measured on a capture. Flat shows it straight on with its own labels, iso at an angle as structure only (it comes apart on request), screenshot puts the keys on the capture itself. Pointing at a part lifts it; pointing at a key fades the rest.

12shader

A WebGL figure drawn by a fragment shader, for what boxes and lines can't show: many things moving at once, a field or gradient, or pixels that are themselves the subject. Follows the theme, pauses off screen, holds a still frame for reduced motion.
