How it works

Gather

Reads the issues, PRs, commits and reviews.

Plan

Picks the few decisions worth a chapter.

Write and check

Draws the figures, writes it up, and checks it.

What it makes

Ask in plain words, inside the repo. The first word picks the output; the post is the default. Ask for a technical deep dive or a release story to write for engineers or for users.

Post

Chapters, drawn figures and real code, as a page you can publish as is or move to your own site.

/buildnotes post how we built preview links

Video

The post as a short video that plays itself.

/buildnotes video tech explainer of our latest PR

Deck

The video as slides you click through, and a PDF.

/buildnotes deck slides on the new billing flow

Figures

Any figure on its own, as an image, GIF, video or SVG.

What it draws

Drawn from measurements, not sketched.
